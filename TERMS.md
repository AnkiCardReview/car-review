## 1. Driving safety comes first
- **You are responsible for driving safely and legally at all times.** Car Review is an audio study aid. It is never a reason to take your attention off the road.
- **Set up before you move.** Open the app, check your cards and tap **Start** while parked. While driving, use only the controls your car provides for audio (steering-wheel buttons) or your headphones' buttons.
- **Never look at, hold or touch your phone while driving.** Mount or stow it as your local law requires.
- **Let cards wait.** If traffic, weather or the road need your attention, ignore the card. Nothing plays until you press a button, and nothing is lost by waiting or stopping.
- **Follow local law.** Some places restrict any phone or headset use while driving, or for new drivers. If using Car Review would break a law where you are, don't use it while driving.
- If a card's content is upsetting or distracting, skip it (double-press ⏮) or stop.

## 2. Studying and your Anki collection
- Car Review applies your car answers through Anki's own scheduler, makes a backup first and lets you undo. Even so, software can have bugs. **Keep your own backups** (Anki does this automatically, and AnkiWeb sync keeps a copy).
- Car Review is a study aid, not medical, legal or professional advice, and it doesn't check whether your cards are correct.
