Car Review

Hands-free flashcard reviews while you drive. This phone reads your due Anki cards out loud, and you answer with your car's steering-wheel buttons.

To connect this phone

  1. On your computer, install the Car Review add-on in Anki (how).
  2. In Anki: Tools ▸ Car Review ▸ Pair phone. A QR code appears.
  3. Point this phone's Camera at the code and tap the link.

No account or app store needed.

Opened from your Home Screen and seeing this?

The icon was added before pairing. Delete the icon, scan the QR code again, and add to the Home Screen from the page that opens.

Before your first drive

How to answer

⏭ Next trackHear the answer
⏭ againGood
⏮ Previous trackAgain
⏮ before the answerRepeat the question
⏮⏮ on a questionGo back a card (from its question)
⏮⏮⏮ on a questionSkip: doesn't work as audio

Works with steering-wheel buttons, AirPods (double-tap = ⏭, triple-tap = ⏮), most Bluetooth headphones, and the ⏮ ⏭ controls on the lock screen. Walking or on a treadmill, you can also use the big buttons on the screen. Nothing plays on its own: after a question it waits as long as you like. After you grade a card the next question starts straight away, and the voice says when a deck is done.

Each drive

  1. Open Car Review before you leave (on Wi-Fi if you can) so it downloads the latest cards.
  2. Connect to the car's Bluetooth. Close Spotify or other music apps, or they may take over the buttons.
  3. Tap Start, lock the phone and put it away. Then drive.
  4. When you arrive, open Anki on your computer. It shows your car answers and applies them.

Safety

Set everything up and tap Start before you move. While driving, use only the steering-wheel buttons, and never look at or touch the phone. If a card needs thought, let it wait; the road comes first. Follow your local laws on phone use while driving.
