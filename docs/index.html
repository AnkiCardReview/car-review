Practice (works like the real app)

Four made-up cards. It reads the question and waits. Say your answer out loud, then:

  • ⏭ Forward: hear the answer (on the two-blank card, one blank per press). After the last answer, ⏭ = Good and the next card starts.
  • ⏮ Back: before the answer, repeats the question. After the last answer, ⏮ = Again.
  • Pressing either button cuts off whatever is playing. Works with the phone locked.

Tests

  1. Screen on: tap Start, then use the ⏭ / ⏮ buttons in Control Center. You should hear "Forward" or "Back".
  2. Screen locked: lock the phone and press ⏭ / ⏮ on the lock screen (or with earbuds). Does it still talk?
  3. After silence: leave it alone for 2+ minutes (watch "Quiet for"), then press again. Does it still respond?
  4. Voice while locked: tap "Read it in 10 s" and lock the phone. Do you hear the sample card? Then switch "How it talks" to Recorded clips and try 1–4 again.
  5. Holding: tick "Also listen for holding a button", then press and hold ⏭ or ⏮. Does anything new appear in the log? (Holding may change what the lock screen shows.)
  6. Car (parked): connect to the Highlander, set it to Bluetooth Audio, start this page and press the steering-wheel buttons. Does the car screen show "Car Review"?

When you're done, tap Copy results and paste them to Claude.
